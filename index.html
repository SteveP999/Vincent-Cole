About the Artist

Vincent Cole

Vincent Cole is candlelit and unhurried, the golden-era sound of Big Band arrangements, American standards, and a voice that belongs in a better decade.

His debut album The Cole Standard is a collection of smoky, sophisticated originals that sound like they were recorded in 1957 and discovered last week. Released through Hello Texas Records.
